# Quick Start - 3 Minutes to Your First Discussion

## What You'll Need
- Chrome browser
- ChatGPT account (https://chat.openai.com)
- Claude account (https://claude.ai)

## Installation (1 minute)

1. Open Chrome and go to: **chrome://extensions/**
2. Enable **"Developer mode"** (toggle in top-right)
3. Click **"Load unpacked"**
4. Select the **`extension`** folder
5. Done! You'll see the extension icon in your toolbar

## First Discussion (2 minutes)

### Step 1: Prepare Your Tabs
- Tab 1: Open https://chat.openai.com and log in
- Tab 2: Open https://claude.ai and log in
- Keep both tabs open

### Step 2: Open the Dashboard
- Click the GPT-Claude Orchestrator icon in your toolbar
- You should see a purple dashboard with a text area

### Step 3: Start a Discussion
- Type a question, for example:
  ```
  Explain the difference between async/await and Promises in JavaScript
  ```
- Click **"Start Discussion"**
- Watch the magic happen!

## What to Expect

1. **Initial Round** (~30 seconds)
   - Both ChatGPT and Claude answer independently
   - You'll see blue (ChatGPT) and pink (Claude) messages

2. **Discussion Rounds** (1-2 minutes)
   - Models critique each other's answers
   - They refine their positions
   - Up to 5 rounds, or until both agree to stop

3. **Final Summary** (~30 seconds)
   - ChatGPT creates a final, comprehensive answer
   - Yellow message with the best insights from both

## Tips

- **Ask complex questions** - The extension works best when both models can contribute unique perspectives
- **Be patient** - Quality discussions take 2-3 minutes
- **Keep tabs open** - Don't close ChatGPT or Claude during a discussion
- **Try different topics** - Programming, philosophy, science, writing, etc.

## Example Questions to Try

**Programming**:
- "What's the best way to handle errors in React applications?"
- "Explain the benefits of functional programming vs OOP"

**Explanations**:
- "Explain quantum entanglement like I'm 12 years old"
- "What causes inflation and how can it be controlled?"

**Decision Making**:
- "Should I learn React or Vue.js as my first framework?"
- "Pros and cons of remote work vs office work"

**Creative**:
- "Help me write a compelling opening paragraph for a sci-fi story"
- "Brainstorm 10 unique startup ideas in the sustainability space"

## Troubleshooting

**"Please open ChatGPT and Claude in tabs"**
→ Open both sites and log in, then try again

**Messages not appearing**
→ Refresh the ChatGPT and Claude tabs

**Extension won't load**
→ Make sure you selected the `extension` folder (not a parent folder)
